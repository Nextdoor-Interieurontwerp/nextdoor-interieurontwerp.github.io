Most of our projects are in Oss, where we are also based — from the stadium skyboxes and the GP surgery to villas, homes and bungalows.

Around it we have worked in Heesch, Schaijk, Uden and 's-Hertogenbosch, all within half an hour's drive.

## Well beyond the region

For the right project we travel further. The Noorderhuis in **Noordeloos** is a good hour away in the province of Zuid-Holland: a multifunctional building for childcare, a primary school, music and village activities, for which we produced the design and all the 3D renders.

In **Houten**, almost an hour from Oss, we designed a modern home for a young family. On request we work across the Netherlands.

[The project in Noordeloos](/en/interieurontwerp-noordeloos) · [The project in Houten](/en/interieurontwerp-houten)

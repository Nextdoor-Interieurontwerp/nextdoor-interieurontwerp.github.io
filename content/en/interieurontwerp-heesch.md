## The Rabobank branch

The Rabobank branch in the centre of Heesch needed a fresh look and a revised layout. The reception desk required a central position to create a welcoming arrival for customers.

After completing the design, NEXTDOOR supervised the furnishing and renovation, to the satisfaction of both staff and customers. A bank branch stays open throughout — scheduling counts for as much as the design there.

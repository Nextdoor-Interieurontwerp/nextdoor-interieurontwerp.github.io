NEXTDOOR is based on the St. Leonardusstraat in Oss. Most of our projects are here — from homes and villas to offices, a GP surgery and the stadium skyboxes.

## Business projects in Oss

For companies and institutions in Oss we delivered the skyboxes and business lounge at the TOP OSS stadium, the reception, waiting room and treatment rooms of the Oost-Brabant out-of-hours GP surgery, and the offices of Acerta Pharma following a complete renovation.

At PivotPark we fitted out 800 m² of offices and laboratories for Aduro Biotech. We also designed the office spaces of Van den Heuvel Makelaars in the town centre and the new premises of Verhoeven Family of Companies.

## Residential projects in Oss

For residents of Oss we designed villas on the Chopinlaan, homes on the Doelenstraat and the Karel Doormanstraat, a house in the Pauwoog neighbourhood, and renovated a bungalow and a semi-bungalow.

That ranges from a complete design — floor plan, 3D visualisation, colour scheme, kitchen, bathroom and lighting plan — through to personal colour advice alone.

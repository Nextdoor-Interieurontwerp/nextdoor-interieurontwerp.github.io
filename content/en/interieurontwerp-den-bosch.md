## Patisserie Limburgia

Patisserie Limburgia's shops sell pies, pastries, cakes, chocolate and artisan ice cream. For the 's-Hertogenbosch location, NEXTDOOR created a robust interior with a bold look.

From 3D design through complete renovation, layout and styling. For a shop open seven days a week, execution matters as much as the design.

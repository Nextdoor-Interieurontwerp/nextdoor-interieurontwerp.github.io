## The Noorderhuis

Interior design for the Noorderhuis in Noordeloos, a multifunctional community building with space for childcare, a primary school, music and village activities.

NEXTDOOR provided the design and 3D renders of both the exterior and the interior of all indoor spaces. In a building used by an entire community, every room has to do something different and still belong to one whole.

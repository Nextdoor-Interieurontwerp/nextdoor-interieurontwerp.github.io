## Home and garden

An integral design for home and garden in Uden. Interior and exterior in perfect harmony, with floor plan, 3D visualisations and personal colour advice.

Design the interior and the garden separately and you see it later. Taking both into one design lets the line run from the living room through to the back of the garden.

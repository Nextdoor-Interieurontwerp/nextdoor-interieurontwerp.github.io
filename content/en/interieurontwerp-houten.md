## A new-build home in Houten

Pim and Lisanne bought a new-build house in Houten and wanted calm influences combined with colourful accents. Their six-year-old daughter loves yellow; their eight-year-old son prefers blue.

Light, spacious and functional, with personal colour advice, children's rooms, a loft and a beautiful herringbone floor. The complete design came together in a presentation folder to go through at home at leisure.
